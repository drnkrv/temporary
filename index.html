About Us

The Support Division handles various functions that provide essential services and assistance to personnel and different units of the organization, including Communications, Recruitment and Administrative Services, Plantilla Affairs, Salary Claims, Assets, and Data Management.

With information, forms, processes, and requests currently coming from different channels, there is an opportunity to establish a centralized and accessible platform where personnel can easily find the information they need, access relevant forms and resources, understand procedures, and connect with the appropriate Support Division focal person.

Support Hub aims to serve as a one-stop portal for Support Division information and services, improving accessibility, coordination, transparency, and overall service delivery.

Mandate

To provide accessible, responsive, efficient, and reliable administrative and support services that enable personnel and organizational units to effectively carry out their respective functions and contribute to overall operational excellence.

Vision

A responsive, connected, and service-driven Support Division where information is accessible, processes are seamless, and every employee is empowered to get the support they need.

Mission

To deliver timely, transparent, and people-centered support services through streamlined processes, accessible information, and responsive assistance—enabling every unit and employee to work more efficiently and effectively.

Core Values

Service: We put the needs of our people and the organization at the heart of everything we do.

Responsiveness: We listen, act with urgency, and provide timely and appropriate assistance.

Transparency: We promote clear information, accessible processes, and accountability in every transaction.

Excellence: We continuously improve our systems, processes, and services to deliver quality results.

Innovation: We embrace technology and better ways of working to make services simpler, faster, and more accessible.

Collaboration: We work across units and functions to provide coordinated and effective support.
